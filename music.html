The first album

সমর্পণ

Shomorpon

Surrender. The giving over of oneself. The record is still being made; two of its songs are already out.

About the record

Songs built the slow way

◆

No two songs on সমর্পণ arrive the same way. About half begin as Nafees's words, with the music built afterwards to carry it; the rest begin as a melody of Syed's that the words are then written into. Either way it then goes to the artists who complete it: they sing it, play it, and give it the form you actually hear. The record is theirs as much as ours.

  • MusicSyed Mehedi Hasan
  • LyricsNafees Fahmi Ali
  • VoiceShanila Islam
  • ProducedSamir Hafiz, who also arranged the record
  • StatusIn progress. Two songs released October 2026.

Why we do it this way

Whoever sings with Ashwin belongs to Ashwin

An artist who gives us a song does not leave as a session credit. They stay as family. The whole point of the project is that the people who lend it their voice and their hands should get bigger for having done so. We would rather build one more career than one more single.
